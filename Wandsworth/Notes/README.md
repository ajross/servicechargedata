Please note that the information provided online states: ‘Services
charges are calculated by a block percentage. This is arrived at on the
basis of the Gross Rateable Value of your property as a proportion of the
total of all the gross rateable values of the properties in the block.’

An example is below:

Apportionment of Service charges for leaseholders

 

Properties will have flat to block percentage and also a flat to estate
percentage if applicable.

 

• The flat to block percentage is calculated pro rata to the Gross
rateable values (GVs) of all the properties in that block

 

• The flat to estate percentage is based solely on the number of flats
and maisonettes on the estate excluding all houses/Bungalows
(properties that could be sold freehold).

 

So, if we have an estate consisting of three blocks as follows

 

Block 1 - 10 flats/maisonettes

Block 2 - 5 flats maisonettes

Block 3 – 5 flats maisonettes

 

The calculation for the estate is based on the number of units in a block
compared to the total number of flats/maisonettes on the estate to come up
with percentage that the block represents of the estate.

So, Block 2 represents 25% of the estate (5/20)

 

And if in Block 2 all properties were identical and had equal GV’s of 200
then each flat would have a flat to block percentage of 20% (200/1000).

 

The flat to estate percentage would then be 5% (flat to block % of 20% x
block to estate % of 25%).
